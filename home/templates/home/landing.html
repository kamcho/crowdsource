Wholesale buying guide

Wholesale imports from China to Kenya

Kenya Imports helps retailers and online sellers source products from Chinese suppliers through group buys. Buyers pool their quantities to work toward a supplier's minimum order quantity (MOQ), then follow the order through import and delivery. Browse available products to see current group-buy details and pricing.

Whether your business is in Nairobi, Nakuru, or elsewhere in Kenya, you can explore the same listed group buys and decide whether a product fits your stock needs. Browse wholesale products.

Looking for dropshipping suppliers in Kenya?

Dropshipping usually means a supplier ships individual customer orders on a retailer's behalf. Kenya Imports currently focuses on pooled wholesale imports, not order-by-order dropshipping fulfilment. If you are sourcing stock for your shop or online business, explore our group buys and check each product's terms before joining.
